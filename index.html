About

Olive is a product manager specializing in AI-enabled products with experience spanning 0 to 1 launches, large-scale platform optimization, and emerging techologies. She leads full-cycle strategy and development for products that enhance efficiency, productivity, and user engagement. As a product manager, she partners with cross-functional teams to transform business and user needs into intuitive digital experiences that drive measurable impact.

Her methodology combines technical frameworks in prompt engineering, fine-tuning, AI evals, agentic workflows and multi-agent architecture expertise. This ensures her solutions are not only technically sound and scalable but also aligned with business objectives. Her approach blends hands-on technical fluency with a focus on usability, impact and speed to market.

Olive is passionate about the intersection of agentic AI, user experience, and business transformation. She continues to build products that make organizations smarter, faster, and more human-centered.

She holds a Master of Science in Computer Information Systems and a Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Engineering.
