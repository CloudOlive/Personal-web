About

Olive leads full-cycle product strategy and development for AI-enabled solutions that enhance efficiency, productivity, and user engagement. As a Product Manager, she partners with cross-functional teams to transform business and user needs into intuitive digital experiences that drive measurable impact.

She specializes in building intelligent agents and AI-powered platforms using AI-assisted development tools for prototyping. This approach accelerates the journey from idea to proof-of-concept, reducing time-to-value while bridging the gap between strategic vision and executable solutions.

Her methodology combines technical frameworks in prompt engineering, fine-tuning, RAGs, AI evals, agentic workflows and enterprise architecture expertise. This ensures her solutions are scalable and aligned with business objectives. Olive is passionate about the intersection of artificial intelligence, user experience, and business transformation. She continues to build products that make organizations smarter, faster, and more human-centered.

She holds a Master of Science in Computer Information Systems and a Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Engineering.
